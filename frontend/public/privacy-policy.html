Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 3, 2026

Introduction

Mosaic by Kaay Labs ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

Information We Collect

When you connect your Jira, Slack, GitHub, or Google Workspace account to Mosaic, we collect:

  • OAuth access tokens to authenticate with your connected services
  • Basic profile information (email, name) from connected services
  • Data you explicitly query through our application (issues, messages, files)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Authenticate you with connected services
  • Retrieve and display data you request from your connected accounts
  • Provide the core functionality of the Mosaic application

Data Storage and Security

We store OAuth tokens securely and encrypted. We do not store the content of your Jira issues, Slack messages, or other data beyond what is necessary for the current session. Access tokens are refreshed automatically and old tokens are discarded.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only used to provide the services you request.

Your Rights

You can disconnect your accounts at any time through the application settings. Upon disconnection, your stored tokens are immediately deleted.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@kaaylabs.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
